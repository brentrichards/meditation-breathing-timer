# Audio credits

Recorded bowl presets use **Tibetan Bowl Struck #1**, sound 1110,
by **Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank**, released under **CC0 (public domain)**.

- Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/tibetan-bowl-struck-s1110.html
- Original MP3: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/1110.mp3
- Licence: https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 6 November 2026.

Bundled derivatives:

- audio/bowl-struck.mp3: first eight seconds.
- audio/bowl-resonant.mp3: eight seconds beginning three seconds into the source.

Both are mono 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3, high-pass filtered at 60 Hz,
normalized to a -22 LUFS target with -4 dBTP ceiling, and faded at the boundaries.
The resonant variant has a slower fade-in.

The **Babbling brook** preset uses **Swale.ogg**, a small swale trickling into
a stream, by **Siddharth Patil (Ksd5 / Sidpatil)**, released under **CC0 1.0**.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swale.ogg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Swale.ogg
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 6 November 2026.

audio/babbling-brook.mp3 is a 20-second excerpt starting at two seconds,
high-pass filtered at 90 Hz, low-pass filtered at 2800 Hz, normalized to
-22 LUFS with a -4 dBTP ceiling, and encoded mono at 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps.
Each breath plays a different portion with the selected phase envelope.
The YouTube reference is not included in the app.

audio/samples.js contains base64 copies of all three derivatives for direct
local-file playback. All other sounds are synthesized by the app.
