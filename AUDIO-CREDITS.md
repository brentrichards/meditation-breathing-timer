by **Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank**, released under **CC0 (public domain)**.

- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 6 November 2026.

Bundled derivatives:

- audio/bowl-struck.mp3: first eight seconds.
- audio/bowl-resonant.mp3: eight seconds beginning three seconds into the source.

Both are mono 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3, high-pass filtered at 60 Hz,
normalized to a -22 LUFS target with -4 dBTP ceiling, and faded at the boundaries.
The resonant variant has a slower fade-in.

audio/samples.js contains base64 copies of these derivatives for direct
local-file playback. All other sounds are synthesized by the app.
